# 9 September 2026 — The home peek floats, and fits the window it opens in

Hovering a canvas card on the home screen grew the card
by the preview's height and pushed the whole grid down — the page jumped
under the pointer, measured at 186px of neighbour shift. The peek had been
deliberately placed in flow ("a popover floating outside would need placing,
and this is a few short rows that the card has room for"), and that
reasoning was reversed the moment it was measured: the peek is now
absolutely positioned, hung off the card's bottom edge over its neighbours,
on the menu's opaque ground, with the card's footprint fixed so the grid
never moves. The DOM position stayed put, so Tab still walks from the open
link through the peek's rows to the ··· row; Escape dismisses, and the
pointer leaving cannot take a preview the keyboard is still reading.

A card near the bottom edge opens its preview upward — but the first cut
chose the side and then applied the stylesheet's fixed 240px cap, which is a
guess about the window. On a short one (measured at 800×300: card 98–213)
the upward peek ran from −94 to 99 — 94px of it above the viewport,
unreadable. The cap is now the smaller of the stylesheet's 240 and the
actual room on the chosen side, less an 8px breath, computed by one pure
function (`peekplace.ts`) the component calls on open and on resize — and
the executing cases in `peekplace.test.ts` replay the measured failure. The
preview scrolls what does not fit, and Tab reaches its links without the
page moving.

Live feedback caught a second visual edge: the card still ended in rounded
corners above a separately bordered preview. The open card and preview now
share the same opaque background and border colour. Their joining corners
are square, the preview covers the card's existing border at the join, and
neither half casts a shadow across the other. The card keeps its border
widths so its footprint does not change. This also works in reverse when
the preview opens upward; cards without a rendered preview stay unchanged.
Verified in a real browser in light and dark themes, at 800×300 and at a
390px width, including pointer entry, Tab, Escape, internal scrolling,
resize while open, and a real click through to an item.

No op, CLI verb, agent-guide, shared-core or README change: this only changes
how existing information is presented on hover or focus.
