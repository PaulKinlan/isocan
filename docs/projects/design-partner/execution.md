cannot authorize execution or establish comparison eligibility. Phase 7 needs a
real, separately runnable baseline, equivalent materialized inputs, a bounded
generation boundary and review instruments before asking for paid runs or people.

authorization to call a provider, recruit participants or change defaults.

## Generation boundary, accounting and recovery

An execution manifest freezes the model/version, harness/revision, tools,
capabilities, source/procedure/package revisions, asset access, repair cap and
per-run token/time/cost limits, plus an aggregate ceiling and explicit
authorization reference. Source/procedure/model/capability mismatches refuse
execution or form a separately named stratum. Never pool them as workflow lift.

The executable boundary must be testable without a provider. Preparation and
instrumentation tests cannot reach paid execution. An actual runner profile is
pinned and read-only preflighted before the provision request; absent flags,
authentication or accounting are reported as unavailable. Provider calls require
the explicit execution mode and authorized frozen manifest. Do not reuse the
separate design-lint pilot's $10 grant, model cap or result rows as authorization.

Journal an attempt and its reservation before launching it. Keep exact run IDs,
prompt/source identities and any unresolved charge on restart; do not rerun an
uncertain call as a new attempt. Enforce absolute deadlines and declared process/
token limits. Record actual usage, estimated/API-equivalent costs separately from
billing evidence, and unknown cost as unknown. Stop further spending on missing
accounting, a mismatched model/tool profile, an overrun or exhausted total budget.
No implicit retries, image calls or extra repair rounds.

Retain failures, timeouts, abandoned and unavailable runs in the denominator.
An incomplete matrix is incomplete. A terminal artifact needs exact stored bytes
and actual source/runtime/build identity. A ready claim requires current task and
viewport evidence for the final output, with known failures resolved. A simulated
provider can prove orchestration and stop rules but cannot make comparison-ready
quality results. Actual source/browser findings and human ratings remain separate.

## Blinded review and analysis

Prepare neutral local review IDs and a separately held condition key. Reviewers
receive the brief, actual final outputs and the same task evidence. Preserve
failed/missing outputs as such instead of dropping their cards. Randomize
left/right placement reproducibly per reviewer and retain ties, both unusable,
disagreement, overall craft score and rubric dimensions. Condition/model/agent
identity must not be exposed by the review wrapper. Never prefill human ratings
or present the builder/conductor's judgment as an independent review.

Use three independent raters and the preregistered five-point rubric. Before
seeing results, fix aggregation: majority pair outcome with ties reported; one
paired comparison per brief/entrance/repetition, not three independent samples
for three votes. Report brief-clustered uncertainty, each entrance and task
category, absolute strong-output coverage, task success and cost/time tails.
Missing and failed candidate outputs count below the craft threshold.

Freeze the exact uncertainty method and seed before paid execution: a deterministic 10,000-resample, 95% percentile interval over brief clusters,
keeping their repeats and entrances together. A nonpositive lower bound is not
evidence for broad default enablement. Use seed `design-partner-brief-bootstrap-v1`. This method is fixed before
paid execution; do not choose a different interval after seeing results.

Provide the six-person partnership protocol and empty recording instruments:
counterbalanced task/condition order, optional realistic cross-entrance return,
questions, steering, rejection of alternatives, facilitator intervention,
acceptable-result time, preference/delegation and reasons. Consent and participant
time are explicit; raw personal data is not committed to synthetic fixtures.
Facilitator work and rating effort are recorded separately from generation spend.

## Release boundary and concrete provision

Prove the existing single `design.workflow` policy enables/disables automatic
enrollment consistently on both entrances while preserving briefs, responses,
choices, drafts and receipts. Reuse the existing switch; do not add a rival flag.
Prepare actual dev/hosted journey and rollback commands. Record missing hosted
identity or authorization precisely; no broad enablement follows local green.

Before asking the user, finish all zero-provider preparation and checks and make
the proposed paid smoke run concrete: 16 A/B generations, four frozen cases, two
entrances, one pinned profile, total/per-run/token/time ceilings, no images or
extra agents, exact stop rule and estimated evaluator effort. The full 96 A/B
and optional 48 C runs follow only after a reviewed smoke result under an
authorized ceiling. Name the six participant sessions and three-rater effort
separately; writing their instruments does not recruit anyone.

Phase 7 stays PART-DONE if money, people, hosted authorization or inconclusive
quality evidence remains. Record preparation as preparation. Do not broaden the
default or close the quality gate merely because all engineering tests pass.
